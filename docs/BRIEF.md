## The eight topics (authored decisions)
1. **Vibe:** "a sea chart on a bright morning". Salt-white paper, deep navy ink, the logo's
   turquoise. References: Admiralty chart paper, a museum label wall, the light in their own
   7m-boat photo off Tel Aviv.
2. **Journey:** the entrance gate (7m) → the real licence (משיט 30) → the honest comparison →
   Greece (open water) → the people (club, founders, reviews) → book a trial sail.
3. **Energy:** light open, steady and dense through the licence, quiet table, then the one big
   open-water moment, warm, then a calm close.
4. **Feeling curve and peak:** below.
5. **One thing no other sailing site does:** it shows you *where on the actual sea* each licence
   lets you sail, and it redraws as you walk past each course.
6. **Aesthetic family:** editorial-nautical, light ground (not dark premium-minimal, not
   cream-and-brass). One hue accent: Sea Time turquoise, two lightnesses (field vs text).
7. **One world or distinct scenes:** distinct objects in a collection (Gallery / catalog).
8. **Assets:** their own photography only (downloaded to `assets/`), their logo, Natural Earth
   coastline (public domain) for the chart. No generation, no kie.ai, no spend.
   Excluded: `ChatGPT-Image-Feb-16-2026…webp` (AI-generated per its own file name).

## Step 1 answers
- **What / for whom:** Tel Aviv sailing school and yacht club; for adults (mostly on phones)
  deciding whether, and how, to start sailing.
- **Believe by the end:** "There is a course here that fits me, and these people will get me
  onto the water."
- **One action, one label:** **תיאום הפלגת ניסיון** (phone call; the inquiry plate also offers
  email and the live contact page). Same label everywhere.
- **Art direction:** photographic world (their photos), plus one drawn object: the chart.

## Feeling curve (one line per act: emotion, then cause)
1. **Invitation**: their 7m boat 46183 pushing in off Tel Aviv, the title sliding behind the
   sail; the chart lights one thin strip of Israeli coast.
2. **Commitment**: the five real steps of משיט 30 travel past right-to-left like a passage plan;
   the chart widens to a 12-mile band along every coast in view.
3. **Clarity** (authored quiet before the peak): the same label schema for all three, set as a
   ledger on paper. No motion except entry. This is deliberate silence, not dead scroll.
4. **Awe (PEAK)**: the paper opens from its centre onto clear Greek-island water; the chart
   swings to the Aegean while the Israeli band goes dark ("בכל העולם חוץ מישראל").
5. **Belonging**: faces. Founders, crews, real Google reviews with names.
6. **Resolve**: an inquiry plate typeset like a chart title block, the course you lingered on
   already filled in, phone number large.

**Peak sentence (visitor to a friend):** "the page opened like a hole in the paper into that
turquoise water, and the little chart swung all the way to the Greek islands."
Peak lives in act 4, gets the largest span (3.4vh), the best photograph, and the quiet ledger
in front of it.

**It's the site where** *a small sea chart shows you exactly which waters each licence lets you
sail, and redraws itself as you scroll past each course.*
